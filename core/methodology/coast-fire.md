# Coast FIRE

## Definition

Coast FIRE is the point at which your investment portfolio has grown large enough that, with no additional contributions, compound growth alone will reach your full FIRE number by your target retirement age. At this stage, you no longer need to save aggressively — you only need to earn enough to cover current expenses.

## Key Parameters

| Parameter | Typical Value |
|-----------|--------------|
| Current Age | 25–45 |
| Target Retirement Age | 50–65 |
| Coast FIRE Number | $100,000 – $500,000 (varies widely) |
| Required Annual Contribution After Coast | $0 |
| Post-Coast Work Requirements | Cover living expenses only |
| Investment Return Assumption | 5–7% real (inflation-adjusted) |

## Core Philosophy

Coast FIRE fundamentally changes your relationship with work. Once you've "coasted," the pressure to maximize income and savings disappears. You can:

- **Downshift** to a lower-stress, lower-paying job
- **Pursue passion projects** that don't maximize income
- **Work part-time** and still reach FIRE
- **Take career risks** (start a business, change fields) without financial anxiety
- **Reduce stress** while maintaining financial trajectory

The key insight: **you've done the hard part** (the early accumulation), and now compounding does the rest.

## Calculation Formula

### The Coast FIRE Formula

```
Coast FIRE Number = FIRE Number / (1 + r)^(Retirement Age - Current Age)
```

Where:
- **FIRE Number** = Annual Expenses × 25 (using 4% rule)
- **r** = Expected real annual return (e.g., 0.07 for 7%)
- **Retirement Age** = Your target age to fully retire
- **Current Age** = Your age now

### Simplified Version

```
Coast FIRE Number = (Annual Expenses × 25) / (1 + Real Return)^Years Until Retirement
```

## Examples

### Example 1: Young Professional

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Current Age | 28 |
| Target Retirement Age | 55 |
| Annual Expenses | $50,000 |
| FIRE Number (4%) | $1,250,000 |
| Expected Real Return | 7% |
| Years Until Retirement | 27 |

```
Coast FIRE Number = $1,250,000 / (1.07)^27
Coast FIRE Number = $1,250,000 / 6.2139
Coast FIRE Number = $201,163
```

**Result**: Once this person has ~$201K invested at age 28, they can stop saving entirely and coast to $1.25M by age 55.

The Coast FIRE number is highly sensitive to assumed returns:

**Important**: Using a lower assumed return (5–6%) is more conservative and accounts for sequence of returns risk.

## The Two Components of FIRE

Coast FIRE highlights a crucial distinction:

### 1. Coast FIRE Number (Achieved)
The amount needed in investments for compound growth to reach your FIRE number.

### 2. FIRE Number (Goal)
The total portfolio needed for full financial independence.

```
Gap = FIRE Number - Coast FIRE Number
```

This gap is bridged **entirely by compound growth**, not by additional contributions.

## Post-Coast Life

Once you've reached Coast FIRE, your financial requirements change dramatically:

### Before Coast FIRE
- Must maximize savings rate
- High-pressure career decisions driven by income
- Every expense scrutinized against savings goals
- Limited career flexibility

### After Coast FIRE
- Only need to cover living expenses (not save)
- Can pursue lower-paying, more fulfilling work
- Career risk becomes affordable
- Mental health improves as financial pressure decreases
- Can take sabbaticals, switch careers, start businesses

## Pros

| Advantage | Explanation |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Reduced financial pressure** | No more aggressive saving required |
| **Career freedom** | Can pursue passion over paycheck |
| **Mental health benefit** | Eliminates the "golden handcuffs" anxiety |
| **Flexibility** | Can still accelerate if desired, but not required |
| **Early checkpoint** | Provides a milestone before full FIRE |
| **Lower risk tolerance needed** | Can shift to more conservative investments post-coast |
| **Life optionality** | Opens doors to entrepreneurship, volunteering, education |

## Cons

| Disadvantage | Explanation |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Still requires work** | You're not retired — just freed from saving pressure |
| **Market dependency** | Relies on compound growth; market crashes extend timeline |
| **Inflation risk** | Spending needs may increase faster than expected |
| **Calculations are projections** | Based on assumed returns that may not materialize |
| **Delayed gratification continues** | Still working, just without the savings pressure |
| **Not true financial independence** | Still need income to cover expenses |

## Who Coast FIRE Is For

- **High-income professionals** who want to transition to lower-stress work
- **Burnt-out workers** who need relief from high-pressure careers
- **Aspiring entrepreneurs** who want to start businesses without savings pressure
- **People who enjoy working** but want to eliminate financial anxiety
- **Career changers** who want to retrain or pivot without income pressure
- **Parents** who want to reduce work hours for family time
- **Anyone who has saved aggressively** and wants to dial back

## Coast FIRE vs. Other Variants

| Aspect | Coast FIRE | Barista FIRE | Regular FIRE |
|--------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Work Required? | Yes (cover expenses) | Yes (part-time) | No |
| Savings Required After? | $0 | $0 | N/A (already retired) |
| Portfolio Needed | $100K–$500K | Higher than Coast | Full FIRE number |
| Timeline to Full FIRE | Fixed (by age) | Extended | Immediate upon hitting number |
| Income Requirement | Cover expenses | Cover partial expenses | None |

## Real-World Example

**Profile**: 32-year-old teacher, $55K salary, $35K annual expenses, $180K invested

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Current Age | 32 |
| Target Retirement Age | 58 |
| Annual Expenses | $35,000 |
| FIRE Number (4%) | $875,000 |
| Expected Real Return | 7% |
| Years Until Retirement | 26 |
| Coast FIRE Number | $167,030 |
| Current Portfolio | $180,000 |
| **Status** | **Coast FIRE achieved** |

**Result**: This teacher has already surpassed their Coast FIRE number. They could:
- Switch to a lower-paying but more fulfilling role
- Reduce to part-time
- Take a year off to travel or study
- Start a side business without income pressure

The compound growth on $180K at 7% for 26 years = ~$1,038,000, exceeding the $875K FIRE number.

## Key Takeaway

Coast FIRE is a **powerful psychological milestone** that transforms your relationship with work. It doesn't mean retirement — it means **freedom from the savings treadmill**. Once you've coasted, every dollar earned is a dollar spent, not a dollar saved. For many people, this is the most meaningful and achievable FIRE milestone.
