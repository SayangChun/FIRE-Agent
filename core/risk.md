# FIRE Risk Management

## Sequence of Returns Risk
Poor returns early in retirement permanently damage portfolio longevity.

```
Portfolio(t+1) = (Portfolio(t) − Withdrawal) × (1 + Return(t))
```
Withdrawal subtracted before return → negatives compound on smaller base. Critical period: first 5–10 years.

Recovery: −30%→+43%, −40%→+67%, −50%→+100%.

**Mitigations:** 2–3yr cash buffer, flexible spending (cut 10–20% early), guardrails (floor 3.5%/ceiling 5.5%), bucket strategy (1–2yr cash, 3–7yr bonds, 8+yr stocks), part-time income 2–5yr, SPIA for essentials, bond ladder 5–10yr.

## Inflation Risk
3% inflation halves buying power in ~24 years.

```
Real Value = Nominal / (1 + inflation)^years
Min Required Return = Inflation + Withdrawal Rate
```
3% inflation + 4% WR → need 7% nominal minimum.

**Hedging:** TIPS (CPI-adjusted principal), I-Bonds ($10K/yr, tax-deferred), REITs/commodities, equity ≥50–70%, TIPS ladder 5–10yr, plan with 3–4% assumption, rebuild FIRE# every 3–5yr.

## Longevity Risk
FIRE retirees need portfolios to last 40–60+ years.

Survival at 4% WR (60/40): 30yr→95%, 40yr→82%, 50yr→70%.

Required WR: 30yr→4.0%, 40yr→3.5%, 50yr→3.0%.

**Strategies:** flexible withdrawals, SPIA 20–40% for essentials, delay SS to 70 (+8%/yr), tax diversification, geographic flexibility, phased retirement (10–15hr/wk), long-term care insurance.

## Concentration Risk
```
Concentration Ratio = Largest Position / Total Portfolio
Portfolio Loss = Position Loss × Position Weight
```

| Level | Ratio | Risk |
|-------|-------|------|
| Diversified | <5% | Low |
| Moderate | 5–15% | Moderate |
| Concentrated | 15–30% | High |
| Highly concentrated | >30% | Very high |

30% position −50% → 15% portfolio loss (vs 2.5% at 5% weight).

**Targets:** no stock >5–10%, no sector >25%, no country >60%. 30–50 stocks for near-max diversification. Allocation: 60–80% equities (60/40 US/Int'l), 15–30% bonds/TIPS, 5–10% REITs. Rebalance quarterly or when drift >5%. Index funds for instant 3,500+ stock diversification.
