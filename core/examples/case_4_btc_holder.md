## Risk Analysis

1. **CRITICAL: Crypto Concentration (76.9%)**: A single −50% BTC drawdown destroys 325,000 in value. This is the dominant risk.
2. **Volatility Risk**: BTC has historically experienced 70-80% drawdowns. This could occur at any time.
3. **Regulatory Risk**: China has banned crypto trading. Holding/liquidation may face legal barriers.
4. **Correlation Risk**: During market crashes, crypto often correlates with equities — no diversification benefit.
5. **Liquidity Risk**: Large BTC positions may be difficult to liquidate quickly without price impact.
6. **Sequence-of-Returns Risk**: If FIRE is reached during a BTC downturn, early withdrawals could deplete portfolio.

### Risk Mitigations
- **Immediate**: Rebalance to ≤30% crypto allocation (sell 325,000 BTC → diversify)
- Build 12-month emergency fund in cash (144,000)
- Convert BTC gains to tax-advantaged accounts where possible
- Consider stablecoin yield as interim parking during rebalancing
- Establish a clear rebalancing schedule (quarterly review)

## Key Takeaway

This BTC holder has a good savings rate (40%) but faces **extreme concentration risk**. With 76.9% in a single volatile asset, the FIRE projection is essentially a bet on BTC appreciation. A −50% BTC crash would set FIRE back a decade. **The single most impactful action is rebalancing** — reducing BTC to 30% and diversifying into traditional assets would dramatically improve the probability of reaching FIRE by 45-50, while still maintaining meaningful crypto exposure for upside.
