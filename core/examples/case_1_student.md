## Risk Analysis

1. **Income Risk**: Starting career, income may fluctuate. Low salary ceiling in early years.
2. **Career Risk**: Job loss or industry downturn could halt savings.
3. **Lifestyle Inflation**: As income grows, expenses may creep up.
4. **Long Time Horizon**: 23 years introduces sequence-of-returns risk.
5. **Low Asset Base**: Minimal cushion for emergencies.

### Risk Mitigations
- Build 6-month emergency fund (30,000) first
- Invest in broad-market ETFs for diversification
- Focus on career growth and income acceleration
- Maintain savings rate discipline as income increases

---

## Recommended Actions

### Priority 1: Immediate (0-3 months)
1. Open a brokerage account and set up automatic monthly ETF investment of 3,000
2. Build emergency fund to 30,000 (roughly 6 months of expenses)
3. Track expenses precisely using a budgeting app

### Priority 2: Short-term (3-12 months)
4. Invest in skills/certifications to accelerate income growth
5. Target monthly income of 12,000+ within 2 years
6. Learn basic portfolio allocation (80% stocks, 20% bonds)

### Priority 3: Long-term (1-5 years)
7. When income exceeds 15,000/month, increase investment contributions proportionally
8. Consider international ETF diversification
9. Revisit FIRE plan annually and adjust projections

---

## Key Takeaway

At 22 with a 37.5% savings rate, this student is off to a strong start. The biggest lever is **income growth** — doubling income within 5 years would dramatically accelerate the timeline. The math works; execution and consistency are what matter now.
